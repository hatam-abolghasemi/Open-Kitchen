# Contributing

Open Kitchen improves through the experience of the brigades that run it.

## Where to start

- **Is a rule right at all?** Open a [discussion](https://github.com/hatam-abolghasemi/open-kitchen/discussions). Debates about direction belong there before they become changes.
- **Ran Open Kitchen?** Share a report in Discussions: kitchen size, what worked, what broke, what you tuned.
- **A concrete problem** (a contradiction, a gap, a broken link, unclear wording)? Open an issue.
- **A ready fix?** Open a pull request. For anything beyond wording, link the issue or discussion it comes from.

## Changes

- Keep the vocabulary: kitchen, dish, guest, cook, counter, brigade, chef, regular, concern, charter, changelog, gateway, family meal, test kitchen, house standards, standards gap. The kitchen is the whole tech organization; the brigade is the DevOps team the model governs; a counter is a station, never a group of people. One thing, one name, everywhere.
- Every change that alters behavior updates [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md).
- A change to a core rule needs a discussion first, because it is a major version for everyone who runs the model.

## The sample house standards

The [manifesto](house-standards/MANIFESTO.md) and [principles](house-standards/PRINCIPLES.md) are the author's own lessons, and they keep changing as those lessons grow. Challenge a belief in Discussions; the author decides what the sample says. If your brigade believes something else, that is not a bug in the sample: write your own standards.

## Versioning

Brigades pin a version in their brigade record and charters, so versions describe what adopters have to do:

- **MAJOR**: a change to a [core rule](MODEL.md#core-rules-and-defaults). Existing brigades and charters have to react.
- **MINOR**: something new and optional: a section, a template, a question, a new default. Nobody has to react.
- **PATCH**: wording and clarity. No change in behavior.

The sample house standards never cause a major version, because no brigade is required to adopt them. A new or changed belief is minor; wording is a patch.

Released tags are never moved.
